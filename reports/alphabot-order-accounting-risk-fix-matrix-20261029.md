## Result and limits

- **Completed:** an explicit, source-linked inventory of known order, fee/accounting and risk fixes with matching isolated GitHub PRs, tests and CI references. For the covered known defects, there are tested fixes without needing to deploy them.
- **Not established:** a comprehensive independent audit of *every* order/adapter path, all historical or present PAPER position quantities, or every safety-limit violation over time. The dedicated Railway production ledger still reported zero auditable fee-adjusted exits at 29 October 2026 16:28 UTC; no completed forward-trade portfolio drawdown or full account reconciliation was verified.
- **Do not claim:** unit test success equals profitable trading, external order success, audited balances, or permission to increase real risk.
- **Production safety:** the connected Railway OAuth app exposes names but not values for `PAPER_TRADING` and `LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED`, so these values are **not independently confirmed** in this review. Runtime PAPER and exchange-write-locked logs are evidence of observed mode, not a substitute for a privileged read-only effective-config audit.
- **Integration check still required:** PR #16 changes are not on the currently running independent shadow branch. Do not merge or deploy solely to improve research scores.

**Bottom line:** Covered isolated defect fixes have tested evidence, and the inventory is complete **for those identified cases**. The project's full completion condition remains unmet until independent data/position reconciliation, holdout/OOS and final validation are documented. No production code was changed by this review.
